That let me design and build solutions from scratch.

I am now CTO at Welyk and was previously Innovation & Emerging Technologies Leader at Dedalus; at night I love writing about the tech world and its evolution, in small bits, on my blog [TheRedCode.it](https://theredcode.it), which I run with the contribution of other authors.

For over ten years I have brought AI, NLP, LLM, DevOps and cloud native topics to the stage, and I have published more than five technical books. My goal is simple: to make technology clear to the people who build it and to those who must choose how to use it.
