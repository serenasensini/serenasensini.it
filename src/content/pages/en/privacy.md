## Data controller

The data controller is Serena Sensini. For any privacy request you can write to [collaborazioni@theredcode.it](mailto:collaborazioni@theredcode.it).

## Data processed

The site has no contact forms and requires no registration. If you accept analytics cookies, Google Analytics collects aggregated browsing data (pages visited, device, approximate region). Without your consent Google Analytics is not loaded.

## Saved preferences

Your consent choice and your light/dark theme choice are stored in your browser (local storage) so they can be remembered on later visits. You can change it at any time from the "Analytics preferences" link in the footer.

## Legal basis and retention

Consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR). Google Analytics data is retained for a maximum of 14 months. Preferences saved in the browser remain until you delete them.

## Transfers

Google may process data outside the European Economic Area, with the safeguards provided by law.

## External links

The site links to third-party services (LinkedIn, GitHub, TheRedCode.it, Emblema, Amazon) that have their own notices. Some book links are Amazon affiliate links.

## Your rights

You may request access, rectification, erasure, restriction and objection, and lodge a complaint with your data protection authority. To exercise your rights write to [collaborazioni@theredcode.it](mailto:collaborazioni@theredcode.it).
